1The journey

Until now, our language models could only read text. In this lecture we teach them to see, to hear and speak, to imagine new images and videos, to act in the physical world — and finally to understand space. Each step builds on the last: first we connect vision and language (CLIP, Flamingo), then we build and train multimodal LLMs, and then we go beyond images to speech, generation, embodied agents and 3D.
